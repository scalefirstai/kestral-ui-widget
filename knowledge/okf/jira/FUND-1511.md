# FUND-1511: Single-fund pricing Validate times out for large funds

**Workaround:** Skip Validate and submit the re-run with the *Validate in batch* checkbox ticked. Tolerance breaches
are then reported on the Pricing Exceptions screen after the job completes instead of up front.

See [Re-run pricing for a single fund](/runbooks/pricing-rerun-single-fund.md).
