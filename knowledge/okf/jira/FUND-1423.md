# FUND-1423: ORA-00060 deadlock in ES_NAV_ACCRUALS

**Summary:** `ES_NAV_ACCRUALS` fails with `ORA-00060: deadlock detected` roughly twice a month when the corporate
actions feed (`ES_CA_INTRADAY`) runs late and overlaps.

**Workaround:** Restart `ES_NAV_ACCRUALS` only, as in the [NAV batch failure runbook](/runbooks/nav-batch-failure.md).
The job is idempotent; a restart does not double-post accruals.

**Root cause:** Both jobs update `ACCRUAL_SCHEDULE` in different row orders. Fix (consistent lock ordering) is
scheduled for Eagle STAR 26.4.
